Platform Features

Everything You Need, All in One Place

A full-stack academic ecosystem built for students, lecturers, and administrators.

Course Management

Browse, enroll, and track courses. Lecturers can publish materials, assignments, and exams.

Assignments & Exams

Submit assignments with file uploads. Take online exams and receive instant graded results.

GPA & Grade Tracking

Automatic GPA calculation based on your enrollment history and grades across all semesters.

Attendance System

Lecturers mark attendance per schedule. Students track their attendance rate and history.

Library System

Browse and borrow books from the university catalog. Track your active loans and return dates.

Clubs & Student Life

Join student clubs, attend events, and engage with campus communities through our student life hub.
